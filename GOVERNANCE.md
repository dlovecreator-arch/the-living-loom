# How the Living Loom remains coherent

The Living Loom is an independent study and creation instrument initiated by Daniel Love with AI assistance. It welcomes close reading, unusual hypotheses, disagreement, and original design. It does not require agreement with a cosmology or claim authority over a reader's identity or experience.

## Evidence and interpretation

Preserve the source before interpreting it. Distinguish an author's words, an observer's description, the author's later explanation, a reader's insight, AI inference, an outside comparison, and a new construction. A source can make an extraordinary claim without this project having independently established it.

Literal, figurative, cryptic, and ordinary readings can coexist. They are not independent confirmations of each other. A proposed cipher needs a constrained, repeatable method and material that was not used to invent the rule. A valuable metaphor need not be called a cipher.

## Who decides what

Daniel maintains the official edition. Contributors own their interpretations and can challenge any project claim. Readers can use a different route through the material without the official record changing to match their preferences. Source authors can correct attribution and explain intention; a later explanation is recorded as a new attributed event rather than silently substituted for the historical artifact.

No contributor submission becomes source truth automatically. Authenticity, rights, context, and the exact claim affected are checked before a correction enters the official edition. Disagreement alone is not misconduct and agreement is not evidence.

## How changes work

A correction identifies the passage or claim, provides the relevant evidence, explains the change, and names the affected material. Transcription errors are corrected with lineage. Interpretive disputes retain serious alternatives when the evidence does not decide between them. A design can be retained as original work even when an earlier source attribution is withdrawn.

Keep source copies and historical decisions recoverable. Current pages display the present state, with a link to substantive revision history. No silent rewriting of frozen predictions or deletion of an awkward result to improve the apparent track record.

## What a finished edition means

A release is finished when its declared scope is assembled, its references and files resolve, its status is accurate, its public boundary is checked, and its reading/use/inspection routes work at the stated verification level. It can contain unresolved questions. It cannot describe missing work as completed.

Baseline completion does not mean that the collection contains every work by Dana, that missing audiovisual channels were recovered, or that a proposed mechanism was experimentally demonstrated.

## When work resumes

Reopen work for a new relevant source, a specific correction, a reproducible failure, a requested application, or a meaningful result from using a design. Address the smallest affected area. Open questions can wait without generating an obligation for perpetual maintenance.

## Public boundaries

The public edition consists of original commentary and method, permitted source references, selected records, and clearly identified demonstrations. Rights in third-party work remain separate. A source unavailable to readers is labeled as limited access. Private records, source reproductions, and active unscored forecasts are not implied contents of the public starter.

The project makes no promise of author endorsement, privileged revelation, or guaranteed personal transformation. Its promise is practical: let readers see the connection, inspect its basis, challenge its limits, and build something new without losing the difference between those acts.
