# Reading protocol

The method has two commitments: read expansively, and preserve enough structure to discover when the reading fails.

## 1. Encounter the artifact locally

Read the supplied words, image, sequence, or audio before importing the dominant model. Record missing channels. A screenshot of a reel is not the reel; one carousel slide is not the carousel.

## 2. Preserve grammar before symbolism

Ask who does what, to what, under which condition, with what scope. Track the object of knowing, remembering, changing, finding, and recognizing. A conditional statement does not automatically establish necessity or exclusivity.

## 3. Hold several modes without merging them

Literal, poetic, symbolic, cryptic, ordinary, and generative readings may coexist. Coexistence is not corroboration. A pun can be useful without being a cipher; a design inspired by a source is not necessarily the source's hidden instruction.

## 4. Extract operations

For a consequential move, name the input, transformation, output, conditions, preserved relations, lost relations, and failure case. Prefer verbs and relationships to a fixed symbol dictionary.

## 5. Retrieve by language and relation

Search for exact wording, then for structurally similar operations under different imagery. Include an awkward or contradictory case when it can change the conclusion. Shared nouns alone are weak evidence.

## 6. State the strongest reading and a meaningful rival

A rival should explain the same observations, not merely express skepticism. Say what future evidence would separate the models. Unknowns remain unknown.

## 7. Type the result

Label each consequential statement as source observation, source-attributed claim, interpretation, outside comparison, Daniel-origin insight, or new Living Loom construction.

## 8. Return to a useful question

The outcome is not always an answer. It may be a sharper distinction, a recovered contradiction, a falsified connection, or a new construction with clear assumptions.
