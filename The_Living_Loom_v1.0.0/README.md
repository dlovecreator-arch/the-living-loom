# The Living Loom

**A way to read complex work closely, trace what makes a connection useful, and build something new without losing the evidence.**

The Living Loom is an independent, AI-assisted study of Dana Sorman's work, initiated by Daniel Love. It follows relationships: what changes, what persists, what opens, what constrains, and which distinctions matter. Literal, poetic, symbolic and cryptic readings can remain alive together.

Choose a way in:

- **See the method:** read [The room, the card, and the hinge](examples/00-inspectable-example.md). The full original example is included, so you can inspect every step.
- **Understand the ideas:** read [Foundations](FOUNDATIONS.md), a connected guide to the Loom's most useful distinctions.
- **Use it:** attach [the portable starter](starter/LOOM_STARTER.md) and your own source to an AI conversation. No installation is required.
- **Explore Dana-derived readings:** follow [the atlas](atlas/INDEX.md) and its worked examples. Their evidence-access limits are stated locally.
- **Build or challenge:** see [use cases](USE_CASES.md), [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.md), and [governance](GOVERNANCE.md).

This is a curated research and commentary edition. It is not Dana's definitive teaching, an endorsed decoding, or the complete source archive. A stable edition can still contain open questions and accept corrections. [Status](STATUS.md) states the exact scope and verification level; [rights](RIGHTS.md) explains reuse boundaries.

The promise: **follow a connection far enough to discover something, and retain the means to find out where it breaks.**
