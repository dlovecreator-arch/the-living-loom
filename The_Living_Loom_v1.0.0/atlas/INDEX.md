# Atlas

The atlas is an orientation, not a required sequence or a complete map of Dana's corpus. For the cleanest first encounter, inspect the fully visible [original demonstration](../examples/00-inspectable-example.md), then read [Foundations](../FOUNDATIONS.md).

- [Memory and continuity](memory-and-continuity.md): what must survive when a state does not.
- [Navigation and addressing](navigation-and-addressing.md): map, path, destination, and access are different.
- [Capacity and selection](capacity-and-selection.md): availability is not uptake; a mixed field requires discrimination.
- [Constraints and emergence](constraints-and-emergence.md): bypass, inversion, clearance, and recomposition alter different things.
- [Roles and distributed function](roles-and-distributed-function.md): a system can act without every participant holding the whole model.
- [Repetition and change](repetition-and-change.md): recurrence can replay, reveal, accumulate, or transform.

The three Dana-derived worked examples remain available in [`examples/`](../examples/). Their evidence-access limits are recorded in the [selected source index](../research/SOURCE_INDEX.md).
