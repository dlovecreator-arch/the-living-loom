# Contributing

The Loom welcomes corrections, rival interpretations, missing-source notices, and design extensions. Agreement with the current model is not required.

## Corrections

Identify the exact file and claim. State what is wrong, the evidence for the correction, and whether the issue is transcription, attribution, chronology, source completeness, or interpretation. A later author explanation is recorded as a new attributed event; it does not silently overwrite the historical artifact.

## Alternative interpretations

Show which observations your reading explains, which relations it preserves, where it differs from the existing reading, and what evidence could distinguish them.

## Source gaps

Describe the missing slide, audio, edit history, date, public locator, or context. Do not upload copyrighted or private material unless you have authority to share it.

## Review flow

Contributions enter a review queue. They do not automatically become source truth, active instructions, or evidence of Dana's intent. Accepted changes receive a revision note and preserve the prior state.
