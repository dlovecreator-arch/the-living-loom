# Capacity and selection

Available information is not automatically usable information. The receiver, interface, timing, and composition of the field all matter.

One source-derived distinction concerns a mixed field: nourishment and debris can coexist, and useful material may be selected before the environment is fully cleaned. That differs from changing how much of a uniform resource can be absorbed.

Boundary: this is not a doctrine that every harmful situation contains a lesson or should be endured. Selective uptake, safe exit, and later cleanup are different decisions.

Open question: which failures come from absent value, which from poor discrimination, and which from an interface that cannot metabolize what is present?
